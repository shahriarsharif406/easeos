            How EaseOS works

            Why

            Planners add screens, badges and choices. On a bad day that is the opposite of help.

            EaseOS does the reverse: you say how much energy you have, and the app gets smaller to match.

            The Friction Meter

            It scores the screen from 0 to 100 across six weighted factors, measured from the live page.

            30·I + 20·W + 15·K + 10·V + 10·C + 15·M, each factor capped at its limit.

            Lenses are approximate

            Low-vision, Dyslexia-friendly, Calm and Keyboard-only views are design aids, not diagnoses. Real experiences vary widely, and nothing replaces testing with disabled people.

            Honest limits

            • Your data stays on this device. LocalStorage only: no sync, no account, and clearing browser data erases it.
            • The score is a heuristic. Fixed, published weights over the live page. It does not measure your fatigue, and the weights are design judgements.
            • Energy is self-reported. EaseOS never guesses how you feel.
            • The meter excludes itself (the ring and its dialog), but counts content scrolled off-screen.

            Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, AI-assisted (see the README). Source on GitHub.

            How is this calculated?

            Six factors, each normalised to 0 to 1 and capped, then weighted. These numbers are live.

            FactorWhat is countedRawCapWeightPoints
            Friction score (sum of points, rounded)0

            score = round( 30·min(I/40,1) + 20·min(W/300,1) + 15·min(K/8,1) + 10·min(V/10,1) + 10·min(C/0.30,1) + 15·min(M/4,1) )

            • Measured from the live page (DOM), every time something changes. The meter itself is not counted.
            • This is a heuristic, not a medical or scientific measurement of fatigue. The weights are design judgements.
            • Not counted: the meter and this dialog, toasts, the skip link and the footer's data controls. Content scrolled off-screen still counts.
            • With reduced motion turned on, the ticking clock and pulses stop, so the High screen scores lower. The Calm lens lowers the score too, because it really removes colour and motion. The Dyslexia and Low-vision lenses barely move it: the meter measures structure, not perception.
